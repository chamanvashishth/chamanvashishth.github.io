02 / About

More interested in understanding than just using.

I learn best by building, measuring, debugging, and explaining what I find.

My interests sit at the intersection of machine learning, mathematics, and practical software engineering. I enjoy going from the underlying idea to a working implementation, then evaluating it instead of treating a successful run as the finish line.

Alongside personal projects, I explore collaborative development through open source: reading unfamiliar code, working with Git, and learning how real projects are maintained.

I'm still early in the journey, and I want this portfolio to reflect that honestly: work in progress, lessons learned, and things I can show rather than claims without evidence.
